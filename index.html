Quick pricing guide for home bakers

  1. Cost every ingredient by weight. A 2 kg bag of flour at $4 is $0.002/g — the calculator does the math.
  2. Pay yourself. Include shopping, baking, decorating, cleanup and packaging time at a real hourly rate.
  3. Add overhead. 5–15% covers oven power, pans, mixer wear and small supplies.
  4. Add profit on top. 20–50% markup is common; custom or decorated work can go higher.
  5. Check your local market, then round to a clean price ($3.50, not $3.37). Don't race to the bottom.
